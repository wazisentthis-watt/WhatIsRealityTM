# NIXO — WIR character and continuity index

**Designation:** Neurological Interface Cross Octoverses (NIXO).  
**Creator:** Wallawazi.  
**Original directive:** Collect → Learn → Return.  
**Body:** biologically grown adaptive Purple Gold, a violet liquid-gel synthetic organism.  
**Identity:** living combat archive, not an automatic power-copy machine.

## Canon locks
- NIXO studies physical and tactical principles: stance, movement, rhythm, prediction, anatomy, environmental force, counter-strategy and changing decisions.
- Copying external movement does **not** automatically grant another fighter's metaphysical energy system.
- Purple Gold can flow, redistribute mass, reconstruct geometry, and regenerate using compatible local energy and matter, but particle-cohesion denial and energy isolation are real counters.
- Persistent star-like chest damage may resist regeneration.
- NIXO's purple octopus neural crown, black/gold trans-adaptive body, violet chest core and long adaptive coat are approved visual elements.
- NIXO is the *active trainee* inside his Morph Mill and Octo Globe, not an observer who sits at a desk.
- **COPY PRINCIPLE / COUNTER PRINCIPLE / IMPROVE PRINCIPLE** is his combat learning record.
- **Subject depleted** means the study is complete, not that an opponent must be killed.

## Professor Punch universe
NIXO also goes by **Dr. Dambé / Professor Punch** in an experimental universe with 118 element-based groups. Ordinary residents have a single element; in this world he commands 18 elemental configurations. A person's emotional surge can push them into elemental form, with form-lock and mental-instability risks. Tuesday and Thursday $1,000 tournaments, political authority, teaching, and the Outlaw Six western confrontation are series premises. Exact 18-element set remains unspecified.
